How it works

One pattern behind my automations.

  1. Incoming information

    Text, voice, email or a form.

  2. AI structures it

    Detects the type and priority, extracts the data you need.

  3. Database or CRM

    Structured data is stored in a database or CRM.

  4. Action and report

    The right person gets an action, the manager gets a report.

Services

  • 01

    Incoming requests → structure and routing

    Form, email, messenger → AI detects the type, priority and data → CRM or database → the responsible person gets a task.

  • 02

    Voice and documents → data

    Voice reports, emails, documents → AI extracts what matters → spreadsheet, database or ERP; anything uncertain goes to a person for review.

  • 03

    Internal team requests

    Employee requests (IT, HR, procurement) → category, priority, owner, deadline tracking.

  • 04

    Reports for managers

    Daily or weekly metrics from a database or spreadsheets → a short summary in Telegram or by email.

Context (simulated)

A premium dental clinic in central Kyiv: 4 dentists, 12 staff, 20–30 appointments a day; the administrator handled 40–50 calls a day manually. Test data and estimates, not a real client.

Task

  • 23 no-shows a month (≈ UAH 80,000 in direct losses).
  • 4+ hours a day on routine calls and reminders.
  • Booking only during working hours.
  • Leads from messengers were getting lost.
  • 34 Google Maps reviews vs 180 for a competitor.

Result estimate on test data

  • No-shows 23 → ~9 a month (−60%), losses UAH 80,000 → ~32,000 a month.
  • 24/7 booking and a 24/7 AI chatbot.
  • Reviews are collected automatically, leads from Telegram no longer get lost.
  • Weekly report.
  • Payback in 1–2 months from no-shows alone (estimate).
More details: 6 workflows, priorities and tests

Solution

6 independent n8n workflows:

  • Reminders: hourly Google Calendar check → email + SMS 24 h and 2 h before the appointment → log in Google Sheets → Telegram alert to the administrator on failure.
  • Cancellations: AI detects the intent to cancel or reschedule → checks booked slots → offers 3 free options → the administrator confirms.
  • Reviews: an hour after the appointment, an HTML email asks the patient to leave a Google Maps review.
  • Leads: an AI bot in Telegram detects the intent, collects name, phone and service → CRM (Google Sheets) → notification to the administrator.
  • Online booking: Typeform → Google Calendar event → HTML confirmation to the patient → notification to the administrator.
  • Weekly report: every Monday at 9:00, a summary in Telegram (appointments, leads, reminders, reviews).

Prioritised by business impact: reminders first (the biggest savings), reporting last.

Tests: full cycle of every workflow; edge cases — no email, invalid phone number, events outside the reminder window, incomplete lead data; Continue on Fail with a Failed status in the logs.

Stack: n8n Cloud, OpenAI GPT-4o-mini, Google Calendar, Google Sheets, Gmail, Telegram Bot API, Turbo SMS, Typeform

How I work

Process and people first, technology second. Every solution comes with tests, error handling and documentation.

  1. Talking to the people who use the process: where time gets lost.
  2. A process map from “as is” to “could be”, and picking the first task with the biggest impact.
  3. A working prototype: n8n, a database, an AI step with a strict response format.
  4. Edge-case tests, error handling and failure alerts.
  5. Documentation and walkthroughs for the team; the final decision always stays with a person.
